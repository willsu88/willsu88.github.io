Cresta Hotel

Room availability, rates, and hotel policies.

Cresta Hotel welcomes bookings and questions from guests and from AI agents acting on a guest's behalf. AI agents can ask about room availability, nightly rates, amenities, check-in/check-out times, pet policy, cancellation policy, and parking.

This site supports the Personal Agent Protocol (Poppy). Agents should discover /.well-known/poppy.json to find the conversation endpoint for Cresta Hotel.
